# 01: Find and verify a usable dataset

**What to build:** Identify and document an appropriate dataset of ordinary single-line Polish or German license plates that can support the student project's detection training and evaluation.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately).

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Verify the dataset's source, actual access method, permitted academic use, country and plate-format coverage, annotation types, and relevant limitations; distinguish verified facts from unknowns.
- [ ] Document steps for teammates to obtain the data, including any required agreement or author contact, without committing images or redistributing them contrary to their terms.
- [ ] Identify how plate text evaluation will be supported, including manual transcription of a small held-out set if necessary.
- [ ] If access remains pending or no candidate meets the requirements, document the blocker and viable next steps without claiming the dataset is available.
